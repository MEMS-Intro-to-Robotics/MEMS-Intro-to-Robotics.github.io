<div class="grid cards" markdown>

    Plan and execute arm motions in simulation using MoveIt 2 and pymoveit2.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Lab 05](lab_05.md)

- **Lab 06 — Pick-and-Place**

    Program a complete pick-and-place pipeline with collision objects and gripper control.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Lab 07](lab_07.md)

- **Lab 08 — Real Drone Control**

    Deploy your tuned controller to a physical Crazyflie drone.
